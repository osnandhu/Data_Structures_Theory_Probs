# Advanced Level (Weeks 9-12+)

**Prerequisites**: Complete all Intermediate topics!

These are the hardest topics but also the most rewarding. These separate good candidates from great ones.

## Topics

### 1. Trees (Weeks 9-10)
- Binary trees, BST, AVL trees
- Traversals: in-order, pre-order, post-order, level-order
- Trie (prefix tree)
- **30%+ of medium/hard problems involve trees**

### 2. Graphs (Week 11)
- Graph representations
- BFS and DFS (both approaches)
- Topological sort
- Union Find
- Shortest path algorithms

### 3. Dynamic Programming (Week 12)
- **Hardest topic** but huge payoff
- Memoization vs tabulation
- State transitions
- Common patterns: Knapsack, LIS, LCS

### 4. Backtracking (Week 13)
- Generate all combinations/permutations
- Constraint satisfaction
- N-Queens, Sudoku solver

### 5. Heaps & Priority Queues (Week 14)
- Min heap, max heap
- Top K problems
- Merge K sorted lists

## Reality Check

- Trees will feel hard at first (everyone struggles with recursion)
- DP is the boss level (expect to spend 2-3 weeks)
- Graphs require visualization (draw them out!)
- These topics require MORE practice than beginner topics

## When You're Ready

✅ Solved 50+ easy and 20+ medium problems
✅ Comfortable with recursion
✅ Can implement data structures from scratch
✅ Understand all intermediate topics well

**Most people spend 2-3 months on beginner/intermediate before starting advanced topics. That's normal!**

## Interview Readiness

After completing advanced topics:
- Aim for 70% of LC Easy solved
- Aim for 40% of LC Medium solved
- Complete 5-10 mock interviews
- Can explain your solutions clearly

**Then you're ready to interview at most companies! 🎯**
