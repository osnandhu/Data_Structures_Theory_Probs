# 12-Week DSA Mastery Roadmap

**Goal**: Go from scared to confident. From zero to software engineer ready.

## Your Mindset Shift

❌ "I'm not good at problem solving" → ✅ "I haven't learned the patterns YET"
❌ "I need to be genius" → ✅ "I need to be consistent"
❌ "Solve everything perfectly" → ✅ "Learn from every attempt"

## The Real Success Formula

1. **Understand the concept** (don't just memorize code)
2. **Implement from scratch** (build muscle memory)
3. **Solve 5-10 problems** (recognize patterns)
4. **Review weekly** (spaced repetition)
5. **Repeat** (consistency > intensity)

## Week 1-2: Arrays & Strings (Foundation)
**Why this matters**: 60% of easy problems use these

### Week 1: Arrays
- **Theory**: How arrays work in memory, time complexity
- **Implement**: Basic operations (insert, delete, search)
- **Solve**:
  - Two Sum (LeetCode 1)
  - Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (LC 121)
  - Contains Duplicate (LC 217)
  - Product of Array Except Self (LC 238)
  - Maximum Subarray (LC 53)

### Week 2: Strings
- **Theory**: String manipulation, ASCII/Unicode
- **Implement**: String operations from scratch
- **Solve**:
  - Valid Anagram (LC 242)
  - Valid Palindrome (LC 125)
  - Longest Substring Without Repeating (LC 3)
  - Group Anagrams (LC 49)
  - Longest Palindromic Substring (LC 5)

**Checkpoint**: Can you explain time/space complexity? YES → Continue

## Daily Practice Schedule

**Monday-Friday** (2 hours/day):
- 30 min: Review theory + implement concept
- 60 min: Solve 1-2 new problems
- 30 min: Review previous problems (spaced repetition)

**Saturday** (3 hours):
- Mock interview: 2-3 medium problems timed (45 min each)

**Sunday**:
- Rest OR review toughest problems from the week

---

## When You Get Stuck (You WILL, everyone does)

1. **Spend 30-45 min trying** (build struggle muscle)
2. **Look at hints only** (not full solution)
3. **If still stuck, watch explanation video**
4. **Code solution yourself** (don't copy-paste)
5. **Revisit in 3 days** (test understanding)
6. **Revisit in 1 week** (solidify pattern)
